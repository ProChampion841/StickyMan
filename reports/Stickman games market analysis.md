# Physics comedy and fairness crown stickman's next hit

In late 2026 the 2D stickman market is huge and evergreen, but few of its games are high quality, and a small developer's opening lies in fixing what players hate rather than inventing a new mechanic. Web portals are dominated by ragdoll-physics fighters with same-keyboard two-player play: **at least seven have each gathered between 190K and 2.7M ratings on Poki or CrazyGames since 2022**, beneath a single one-button giant, Madbox's Stickman Hook, with about **7.9M Poki rating votes**. Mobile install charts still belong to 2016–2019 incumbents (Stickman Party at 500M+ Google Play installs; Stick War: Legacy, Supreme Duelist and Stickman Hook at 100M+ each), while new money flows to idle-RPG hybrids such as Epic Stickman and new growth to ragdoll sandboxes. On Steam, the winners are cheap premium games built on physics chaos (Stick Fight, Stick It to the Stickman) or written comedy (The Henry Stickmin Collection, 98% positive). Across the big free games, players' loudest and most consistent complaint is monetization: intrusive ads, gem walls and $7.99-a-week subscriptions. The clearest gaps are a real online physics brawler on mobile (empty since Stick Fight Mobile was pulled after roughly 22M Android downloads), mobile and web versions of the premium physics-roguelite and comedy-adventure formulas, and any game that pairs ragdoll mechanics with a strong writer's voice. A solo developer's best play is a physics-driven game built on one action the player understands instantly, loud but readable hit feedback, failure played for comedy, and same-device multiplayer, shipped as a tiny web build (Phaser 4 with Box2D v3 or Rapier, or Defold) to Poki first and then grown into a premium Steam release and a fairly monetized mobile port. Treat every number here as directional: portals publish votes rather than plays, and third-party revenue estimates for the same game routinely differ by two to four times.

Web portals give away discovery but pay only in ad revenue; mobile stores rank games by accumulated ratings and paid user acquisition; and Steam is where a few cheap premium games earn the genre's largest verifiable sums. ### Web portals reward one-action physics and same-keyboard duels

Poki's stickman category lists 69 games, and a handful of them dominate ([Poki](https://poki.com/en/stickman)). **Stickman Hook holds 4.4 stars from 7,918,244 votes**, more than three times the next title, and was still being updated in September 2025, almost seven years after release ([Poki](https://poki.com/en/g/stickman-hook)). Behind it sit Henry Stickmin's **Fleeing the Complex at 4.7 from 2.42M votes** ([Poki](https://poki.com/en/g/fleeing-the-complex)), the merge shooter Stick Merge at 1.9M ([Poki](https://poki.com/en/g/stick-merge)) and War of Sticks, a game in the Stick War mold, at 950K ([Poki](https://poki.com/en/g/war-of-sticks)), with a dense pack of ragdoll fighters below. Poki doesn't rank purely by votes: the brand-new Mad Stick (September 2026, 8.7K votes) sits seventh and GladiSticks, with 75 votes, tenth, consistent with Poki's statement that it limits daily releases "so each game gets a real shot at being seen" ([Poki: Mad Stick](https://poki.com/en/g/mad-stick); [Poki Developer Guide](https://developers.poki.com/guide)).

CrazyGames tells the same story with a different leader. **Ericetto's Ragdoll Archers has 2.5M likes against 223K dislikes and rates 9.1/10** ([CrazyGames](https://www.crazygames.com/game/ragdoll-archers)), followed by the Unity-built Stickman Clash, with 662K likes and still updated in September 2026 with 3–4 player modes planned ([CrazyGames](https://www.crazygames.com/game/stickman-clash)), and GamePush's Stickman Kombat 2D, rated 9.0 with 267K likes ([CrazyGames](https://www.crazygames.com/game/stickman-kombat-2d)). The 2009 Flash original of Stick War, now running through the Ruffle emulator, still rates 9.4 ([CrazyGames](https://www.crazygames.com/game/stick-war)). CrazyGames itself says the category "mostly contains fighting, 2D platformer, and shooting games, along with some two-player titles" ([CrazyGames](https://www.crazygames.com/t/stick)).

These numbers trace a single winning template for 2023–2026: a ragdoll stickman, a combat or toss action readable within seconds, weapons and skins as progression, and **local two-player on one keyboard**. Nearly every recent fighter ships that last feature — Ragdoll Archers splits WASD against the arrow keys, Stickman Kombat 2D splits WASD against IJKL — because it needs no netcode and suits classroom and couch play ([CrazyGames: Ragdoll Archers](https://www.crazygames.com/game/ragdoll-archers); [CrazyGames: Stickman Kombat 2D](https://www.crazygames.com/game/stickman-kombat-2d)). Ericetto shows the template can be repeated: Ragdoll Archers (2023), Ragdoll Hit (2024) and Ragdoll Drop (2025) are three portal staples from one small developer. New entrants still break through — **Stickman Battle collected 410K votes in about 11 months** — but Poki states flatly that "clones of existing hits don't stand out to players and won't be promoted" ([Poki: What we look for](https://developers.poki.com/guide/what-we-look-for)). The ragdoll fighter is now the price of entry, not a point of difference.

itch.io and Newgrounds matter less as markets than as test labs and as the genre's history, and both were hard to measure for this report: itch.io shows no play counts, and Newgrounds pages could not be retrieved. itch.io's free web stickman and ragdoll listings are mostly small experiments such as Stick Slasher, Ragdoll Cliff Diving and the two-player Fallers ([itch.io stickman](https://itch.io/games/free/platform-web/tag-stickman); [itch.io ragdoll](https://itch.io/games/free/platform-web/tag-ragdoll)). Newgrounds is where the genre's classics were born: Xiao Xiao's stick-figure kung fu in 2001–02 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xiao_Xiao)); **Stick RPG, which drew one million plays in its first month in 2003** and founded XGen Studios ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/XGen_Studios)); Henry Stickmin from 2008 ([Meme.com](https://meme.com/memes/henry-stickmin)); and Stick War in 2009 ([Stick War Fandom](https://stick-war.fandom.com/wiki/Brock_White/whiting39)). Fancy Pants Adventures World 2 alone logged about 14.8M plays on Armor Games and 17.8M on Addicting Games by 2011 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fancy_Pants_Adventures)). Where each classic's line went next is revealing. Stick War continues in War of Sticks and Stick War: Legacy, and Xiao Xiao in today's ragdoll fighters, but **Henry Stickmin's choose-your-path comedy and Stick RPG's open sandbox have no new successor**; the originals are simply re-hosted.

Portal economics shape what a solo developer should build. Poki reports **100M monthly players** and pays 50% of ad revenue on traffic it sends and 100% on traffic you bring yourself, in return for a five-year web-only exclusive that explicitly leaves Steam, mobile stores and consoles open ([Poki for Developers](https://developers.poki.com/); [Poki Developer Guide](https://developers.poki.com/guide); [Poki deal types](https://developers.poki.com/guide/revenue-deal-types)). It bans in-app purchases and paid ad removal, forbids rewarded ads from gating core progress, warns that players leave if loading takes more than 10 seconds, and describes its audience as "mostly mobile," so "a desktop-only game is at a disadvantage" ([Poki requirements](https://developers.poki.com/guide/requirements-quality); [Poki: What we look for](https://developers.poki.com/guide/what-we-look-for)). CrazyGames is non-exclusive but gated on metrics: a new game first gets a 7–21-day Basic Launch with no ads and must reach at least 500 plays and beat benchmarks for playtime and retention before Full Launch unlocks monetization, and games whose initial download exceeds 20 MB are kept off its mobile homepage ([CrazyGames docs](https://docs.crazygames.com/); [CrazyGames technical requirements](https://docs.crazygames.com/requirements/technical/)). Hard revenue data is scarce. Secondary guides put a solid casual portal game at **$200–$2,000 a month** ([Cinevva](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/web-game-monetization)), and the engine maker Defold reports that games built with it earned **€3M from 600M+ plays on Poki in 2024** — roughly €5 per thousand plays across a whole portfolio, though that is the vendor's own figure ([Defold](https://www.defold.com/gamescom2025/)). Web stickman is a discovery engine and a playtest lab more than a cash register.

Revenue tells a different story from installs, and it needs careful reading. Sensor Tower's public figures count store purchases only, so ad-led games look tiny there — Supreme Duelist earned about $254 a week in US purchases and Stickman Hook about $862 — while AppGoblin's model, which includes ads, puts each at **$500K+ a month**, with 73% of Stickman Hook's revenue from ads and an estimated 6.8M monthly users ([Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q4-unified-top-5-stickman-games-units-us-60326a29241bc16eb8b4f0b6); [AppGoblin: Stickman Hook](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.mindy.grap1); [AppGoblin: Supreme Duelist](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.Neurononfire.SupremeDuelist)). The purchase leader earns in a different way entirely. **Epic Stickman: Idle RPG War peaked near $68.8K a week in US store revenue in late December 2025**, about four times Stick War: Legacy's $17.7K peak, earning roughly 70% from purchases and 30% from ads ([Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q4-unified-top-5-stickman-games-revenue-us-60326a29241bc16eb8b4f0b6); [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.fansipan.epic.stickman.survival.rpg.idle.game)). Over a full year that peak would come to about $3.5M in US purchases, though this extrapolates from a holiday high rather than measuring anything. Bowmasters, the archery-duel standard, was estimated at more than $4M gross in a single quarter as far back as 2018 ([Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/playgendary-revenue-q3-2018)).

The leaders share one monetization stack: free-to-play, rewarded video and interstitial ads, a $2.99–$3.99 remove-ads purchase, gems, chests and starter packs. Stick War: Legacy is typical, selling gem packs from $2.99 to $27.99, Golden Chests up to $24.99 and bundles up to $33.99, while dropping new Missions every Friday as ongoing live content ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/stick-war-legacy/id1001780528)). Bowmasters goes furthest, with a **$7.99-a-week subscription** after a three-day trial ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bowmasters/id1118431695)). Where the audience lives caps what ads alone can earn: rewarded video paid about $24–30 per thousand views in the US in Q2 2024 but roughly $2–3 in Latin America ([GameDev Reports/Tenjin](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/tenjin-ad-monetization-in-mobile-00b?open=false); [Playio/Appodeal](https://blog.playio.co/mobile-game-ecpm-benchmarks-2026)), and some stickman hits draw heavily from emerging markets — Painty Hide n Seek's traction is concentrated in Indonesia, India, Vietnam, Brazil and Turkey ([Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/top-10-worldwide-mobile-games-by-revenue-and-downloads-in-july-2026)).

No publisher dominates. The top earners and download leaders are mostly single-IP or small-portfolio studios — Max Games, Neron's Brother, PlayMax, Madbox — while big hyper-casual houses such as Voodoo, SayGames and Lion Studios own one or two stickman-adjacent hits each, and Asian reskin clusters such as Fansipan and Pusilung churn out near-identical idle RPGs ([Google Play search](https://play.google.com/store/search?q=stickman%20idle%20rpg&c=apps&hl=en_US&gl=US)). The 2024–2026 movers fall into four groups. Idle-RPG hybrids lead on money: Epic Stickman reached 10M+ in June 2026 and is still adding 8% every 60 days. Ragdoll fighters and sandboxes lead on fresh installs: Stickman Ragdoll Fighter: Bash reached 50M+ in June 2026, and a Ragdoll Hit listing grew from 100K to 5M+ installs in two years. Online PvP with fair positioning is growing too, led by Stick War: Saga, marketed as "no Pay for Power" and up 12% per 60 days ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/stick-war-3/id1548764863)). And 3D "stickman world" casual games built on paid acquisition can spike fast without being loved: Painty Hide n Seek went from 1M to 5M installs in a month despite a 2.8-star rating. The wider market leans the same way. AppMagic found that **action-game revenue fell 24.5% in 2025** while strategy revenue rose 15.9% and hyper-casual purchase revenue rose 80% as the genre bolted on RPG-style progression ([GameDev Reports/AppMagic](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/appmagic-mobile-market-landscape)).

### Steam pays for premium chaos and written comedy

Steam's stickman winners are few, cheap and premium. **Stick Fight: The Game** ($4.99, 2017) holds 94% positive across 52,654 English reviews and, eight years after launch, still drew 247 reviews at 92% in the last 30 days ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/674940/Stick_Fight_The_Game/)). It had sold more than 2M copies by 2019 ([Triple Point PR](https://pressreleases.triplepointpr.com/2019/07/23/stick-fight-game-mobile-available-now-netease-landfall/)); third-party trackers now range from SteamSpy's 2–5M owners to SteamPulse's 7.4M units, with gross revenue estimates of $21M–$26M ([SteamSpy](https://www.steamspy.com/dev/Landfall+West); [Raijin](https://raijin.gg/app/674940/Stick_Fight_The_Game); [SteamPulse](https://steampulse.org/game/674940)). **The Henry Stickmin Collection** ($14.99, 2020), published by Innersloth, the studio behind Among Us, is "Overwhelmingly Positive" at 98% of 40,955 English reviews ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1089980/The_Henry_Stickmin_Collection/)), with estimates running from about 1.1M copies and $10.3M gross up to 4.3M copies ([Raijin](https://raijin.gg/app/1089980/The_Henry_Stickmin_Collection); [VaporLens](https://vaporlens.app/app/1089980/the_henry_stickmin_collection/stats/details)). The 2025–26 breakout is **Stick It to the Stickman** from Free Lives and Devolver. It entered Early Access in August 2025 at about $5, was #1 on Steam by units sold in March 2026, and reached 1.0 on 23 September 2026 at $11.99, with 95% positive of 5,199 English reviews and an estimated 264K–349K copies sold ([Games Industry Africa](https://gamesindustryafrica.com/2025/08/25/south-african-studio-free-lives-launches-stick-it-to-the-stickman/); [GIA March 2026](https://gamesindustryafrica.com/2026/04/08/gia-monthly-unit-sales-overview-for-march-2026/); [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2085540/Stick_It_to_the_Stickman/); [Raijin](https://raijin.gg/app/2085540/Stick_It_to_the_Stickman); [SteamPulse](https://steampulse.org/game/2085540)). Its recent-review score has slipped to 81% since 1.0, and no source yet explains why.

Two lessons follow. First, Steam's long tail of $0.99–$2.99 "Stickman X" titles crowds the search results but gets little traction ([Steam search](https://store.steampowered.com/search/?tags=&term=stickman&sort_by=Reviews_DESC)); the three hits succeed on identity, not on the word "stickman," which two of them don't even use in their names. Second, Steam is where web game series turn their fans into sales. Henry Stickmin went from free Newgrounds games to a $14.99 collection, Fancy Pants went from Flash portals to Super Fancy Pants Adventure on Steam in 2017 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fancy_Pants_Adventures)), and Poki's exclusivity terms are written to permit exactly that path.

How far to trust these figures varies by source. Poki and CrazyGames show rating votes rather than play counts, so web reach is inferred from votes; Newgrounds and itch.io detail pages could not be retrieved; Google Play figures come from AndroidRank's mirror of the store's install brackets; and Steam sales figures come from trackers that disagree with each other by up to fourfold. No public source reports retention, revenue per daily user or total revenue for any web stickman hit. The rankings above are reliable; the dollar figures give only the order of magnitude.

## Winning stickman games share one action, loud impacts and funny failure

The documented hits follow two business models with one shared design core. Cheap free games built on one mechanic win portals and app stores (Stickman Hook, Bowmasters, Shadow Fight 2), and cheap premium games win Steam through social chaos or comedy (Stick Fight, Henry Stickmin). Both kinds started tiny. Stick Fight began as a one-person game-jam project and became a commercial game after **about three months of late nights and weekends by four people**, reusing the physics system from Landfall's Totally Accurate Battle Simulator ([Landfall press kit](https://landfall.se/stick-fight-press-kit)).

**The more mass-market the stickman hit, the fewer simultaneous inputs it needs.** Stickman Hook uses a single input — hold the mouse button, space bar or screen to attach, release to launch — that works the same on every platform, and most attempts end in under five seconds ([Poki](https://poki.com/en/g/stickman-hook)). Bowmasters uses one drag-to-aim gesture ([Multiplayer.it](https://multiplayer.it/recensioni/173559-bowmasters-angry-bows.html)), and Henry Stickmin uses point-and-click choices that need no dexterity at all ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1089980/The_Henry_Stickmin_Collection/)). Deeper fighters pay a price: Shadow Fight 2's left joystick plus punch and kick buttons yields dozens of moves, but Gamezebo faulted its "imprecise controls," and Nekki added layout customization only later ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shadow_Fight_2)). Hitstop — a brief freeze on impact — helps here, because the frozen frames widen the timing windows for cancels and combos; that is why Street Fighter II's two-in-one cancel exists ([critpoints](https://critpoints.net/2017/05/17/hitstophitfreezehitlaghitpausehitshit/)).

A stick figure carries no surface detail, so motion, timing, camera, color and sound must carry all of the feedback. Landfall says flatly that "physics and destruction look inherently satisfying on screen" ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/how-i-tabs-i-creator-landfall-got-discovery-right-)). The best-documented rules for hits come from Masahiro Sakurai, the Super Smash Bros. director: **hitstop should scale with damage up to a cap, the victim should shake during the freeze, and freezes should be shorter in multiplayer**, because a long shared freeze lets a third player exploit the frozen pair ([Source Gaming](https://sourcegaming.info/2015/11/11/thoughts-on-hitstop-sakurais-famitsu-column-vol-490-1/)). Fighting-game benchmarks run from roughly 9 frames of freeze for light hits to 13 for heavy ones ([Sonic Hurricane](https://sonichurricane.com/?p=1043)), and tutorial guidance puts 50–100 ms on heavy hits as the point where an impact starts to read as "real force" ([Wayline](https://www.wayline.io/learn/game-feel/1)). When Henry Stickmin's creators remastered the series, they spent the budget on reanimation and new sound effects rather than new systems ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1089980/The_Henry_Stickmin_Collection/)). The counterweight is readability: Folmer Kelly argued in a GDC Europe talk that piling on impact effects can cost immersion ([RPG Playground](https://rpgplayground.com/research-making-a-juicy-game/)), a real risk in four-player brawls.

Physics is both the gameplay and the marketing. Stick Fight's combat is "physics-based, with procedural animations from Totally Accurate Battle Simulator's system" ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/674940/Stick_Fight_The_Game/)), and Landfall credits physics that are fun to watch for how its games spread: "When seeing a gif or a video of the game, you instantly get a taste of what playing the game will be like" ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/how-i-tabs-i-creator-landfall-got-discovery-right-)). Hand-keyed animation wins instead where comic timing has to be written in, as in Henry Stickmin and Alan Becker's Animator vs. Animation. Stickman Hook sits between the two, with pendulum physics so precise that releasing "even half a second late" kills forward speed ([Poki](https://poki.com/en/g/stickman-hook)). The structural advantage for a small team is that a stick figure is the cheapest possible ragdoll, about 10–15 bodies and a dozen joints, so procedural and physics-driven animation cost far less than they would for detailed characters.

Three progression patterns recur. Stickman Hook runs a level ladder with cosmetics: hundreds of short levels, a second mode unlocked at level 30, and 23 skins, fourteen of which unlock by watching ads, plus weekly limited-time skins as a cheap reason to return ([Poki](https://poki.com/en/g/stickman-hook)). Shadow Fight 2 runs an RPG gear ladder with bosses and an energy gate of five fights per refill; CNET praised the game while flagging its "in-your-face freemium model," and Nekki later sold a paid Special Edition for players who reject that friction ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shadow_Fight_2)). Stick Fight has **no single-player mode and no unlock system at all**, keeping players through friends and over 100,000 community-made levels ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/674940/Stick_Fight_The_Game/)). Set expectations against real benchmarks: across 16,262 mobile games in 2025, median day-7 retention was just under 4%, while the top quarter reached just above 30% on day 1 and 6–7% on day 7 ([GameDev Reports/GameAnalytics](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gameanalytics-mobile-and-pc-game)). Arcade-style games typically lead on day-1 retention but fade over the long term ([AppAgent](https://appagent.com/blog/mobile-game-retention-benchmarks/)).

Standout stickman games pick one visual system instead of adding detail: Shadow Fight's dark silhouettes on vivid backgrounds, Bowmasters' cute characters with "Mortal Kombat-styled gore" ([GamerBraves](https://gamerbraves.com/?p=588)), Stick Fight's "iconic stick figures from the early internet" ([Landfall](https://landfall.se/stick-fight-press-kit)). The minimal body also makes cosmetics nearly free — Stickman Hook's skins include a burger, a cactus and a lemon. Story works through failure. Henry Stickmin's Steam tagline is "A choose-your-own-path where failing is more fun than succeeding," and the remaster even retrofitted fail screens into the first game ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1089980/The_Henry_Stickmin_Collection/); [Newgrounds Wiki](https://newgrounds.wiki.gg/wiki/Breaking_the_Bank)). Fail scenes like the "Distraction Dance" became memes in their own right ([Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/subcultures/the-henry-stickmin-collection)), and the fourth-wall "creator versus creation" premise powers Alan Becker's channel, which passed 30M subscribers in February 2025 according to fan-maintained wikis ([AvA Wiki](https://animatorvsanimation.fandom.com/wiki/Alan_Becker_(YouTube_channel))). Stick It to the Stickman brings these threads together: a satirical climb up a corporate tower to CEO, 21 employee archetypes with unique attacks, "gooey" physics combat, couch co-op, and a level editor with Steam Workshop support ([Rogueliker](https://rogueliker.com/stick-it-to-the-stickman-leaves-early-access/); [The Indie Informer](https://the-indie-in-former.com/2025/08/25/stick-it-to-the-stickman-impressions-when-the-stickman-comes-around/); [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2085540/Stick_It_to_the_Stickman/)). Landfall's marketing playbook adds the distribution layer: free launches to fill multiplayer lobbies (Totally Accurate Battlegrounds drew about 3M downloads while free for 100 hours) and an annual April Fools' release ritual ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/how-i-tabs-i-creator-landfall-got-discovery-right-)).

## Players punish greed, and three premium slots sit empty

The critical reviews of every major free mobile stickman game repeat one grievance: monetization. Stick War: Legacy players write that "everything costs so much gems and u only get 5 each" and that weekly levels stay locked unless you pay "twelve dollars" ([App Store reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/stick-war-legacy/id1001780528?see-all=reviews)). Bowmasters reviewers complain that "ads pop up every two minutes," that "a game is never worth $8.00 a week," and that ads show modes that aren't in the game ([JustUseApp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1118431695/bowmasters-multiplayer-game/reviews)). Stickman Legends players object to "a TON of forced ads," a $10 ad-removal bundle they want split into an ads-only option, PvP opponents "like level 500 and higher," and server errors that lose progress ([JustUseApp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1186523572/stickman-legends-offline-games/reviews)). A 2019 children's-media review rated Stickman Hook for "saturated advertising," with unskippable 20–30-second videos, while finding "no concerns with the game itself" ([ACCM](https://childrenandmedia.org.au/app-reviews/apps/stickman-hook)). Headline ratings stay at 4.5–4.8, so the anger lives in the critical tail, and many "too hard" complaints are economy complaints in disguise: players blame gem walls and boss timers, not their own skill.

The PC and "multiplayer" leaders fail differently. Stick Fight's negative reviews center on unplayable public lobbies, cheaters with no effective anti-cheat, years without updates, desync, and the absence of bots for solo players ([VaporLens](https://vaporlens.app/app/674940/stick_fight_the_game)). Supreme Duelist, the top US stickman download, offers PvP only "against friends or bots," with no true online play ([RuStore](https://www.rustore.ru/catalog/app/com.Neurononfire.SupremeDuelist)).

A new game cannot be found by searching the bare keyword. On Google Play, "stickman" carries a keyword difficulty of 90 with more than 250 ranking apps, 195 of them with over 1,000 reviews, while longer phrases such as "stickman fight" (difficulty 30) and "henry stickman" (21) are far easier ([ASOTools](https://asotools.io/app-store-keywords/stickman)). Rating volume protects the incumbents: no newcomer will outrank Stick War: Legacy's 3.2M Play ratings at 4.82 stars on the head term. The "henry stickman" query is telling — it has real search volume even though no official Henry Stickmin mobile game exists, so clones capture demand for a missing premium brand. Knockoffs also target new hits: an unaffiliated "Stick It to the Stickman" Android app installed an unrelated jigsaw game ([itch.io](https://itch.io/post/5027383)).

Three gaps are backed by direct evidence. The first is a **real online physics brawler on mobile and web**. Stick Fight: The Game Mobile, NetEase's online 2–4 player port, reached about 22M Android downloads before it was pulled from Google Play in July 2023 ([AppBrain](https://www.appbrain.com/app/stick-fight-the-game-mobile/com.netease.ddsfna)), and TapTap players still ask why "one of the craziest and most luxurious 2D games ever" disappeared ([TapTap](https://www.taptap.io/app/168336)); no official replacement exists. The second is the **premium physics-roguelite formula on mobile**: Stick It to the Stickman is Windows and macOS only, and when players asked about mobile its developer said there were no plans "at this stage" ([itch.io](https://itch.io/post/4155575)). The third is **comedy adventure on mobile**: Henry Stickmin has no official mobile release, and an unofficial fan port on itch.io draws reports of crashes and black screens — demand without supply ([itch.io](https://puffinplayappstore.itch.io/henry-stickmin)). Weaker but plausible openings include a stickman metroidvania (a search found none from 2024–2025, only roguelite brawlers and arena shooters; [Rogueliker](https://rogueliker.com/stick-it-to-the-stickman-leaves-early-access/); [Deku Deals](https://www.dekudeals.com/items/stickmans-arena)), level editors on mobile (every editor found in a hit is PC-only), cross-platform web-plus-mobile multiplayer (no example found at scale), and a successor to Stick RPG's open-ended sandbox. By contrast, generic 1v1 duel games, rope-swing hyper-casual games, dismount physics, crowd runners and idle "legends" reskins are saturated — and they generate most of the complaints above.

The strongest counter-argument is that the money is in idle RPGs and paid user acquisition, not in fairness. Epic Stickman does out-earn everything else on US purchases. But that segment is a reskin business: Fansipan and Pusilung each run several near-identical titles, **56% of top-grossing games' ad creatives were AI-generated in 2025**, and only about 10% of the 1.4M apps released that year drew any attention ([GameDev Reports/AppMagic](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/appmagic-mobile-market-landscape)). New hyper-casual hits now need day-30 retention of 5–7.5% and roughly 500 levels at soft launch ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/what-happened-to-hypercasual-the-markets-evolution-over-the-past-year/)) — a bar that suits a funded studio, not a solo developer. The evidence for the alternative is broader: Stick It to the Stickman succeeded with no ads at all, Stick War: Saga is growing on a "no Pay for Power" pitch, and premium stickman games earn 95–98% approval. All of it points to fairness and identity as the realistic edge for a small team.

## Small web builds and Box2D-class physics beat heavyweight engines

Platform rules largely make the technology decision for you. Poki loses players after 10 seconds of loading and asks for a small file size (an older Poki page set an 8 MB initial-download target); CrazyGames requires 20 MB or less for its mobile homepage and **disables Unity games on iOS by default** because of memory crashes; and both forbid external requests ([Poki requirements](https://developers.poki.com/guide/requirements-quality); [Poki Unity SDK](https://sdk.poki.com/unity); [CrazyGames technical requirements](https://docs.crazygames.com/requirements/technical/)). Empty web builds differ by an order of magnitude. Defold's own chart puts Defold at 0.83 MB, Godot at 6.68 MB and Unity at 11.73 MB ([Defold](https://www.defold.com/gamescom2025/)), and an independent guide measured Unity's 2D template at about 7.7 MB compressed, or roughly 2 MB with aggressive stripping ([Cinevva](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/godot-vs-unity-web-games)). A JavaScript framework with procedurally drawn stick figures leaves almost the entire size budget for audio and content.

| Option | Language | Empty web build | Licence cost | Ragdoll physics | Best fit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Phaser 4 + Phaser Box2D | JS/TS | Smallest class; Box2D port about 65–70 KB | Free (MIT) | Box2D v3 API with revolute motors | Web-first, then Capacitor to app stores |
| PixiJS v8 | JS/TS | Small (renderer only) | Free | Bring your own (Rapier, Box2D) | Custom engines, WebGPU |
| Defold | Lua | About 0.8–1 MB | Free | Box2D v3 opt-in; joint API in 1.13 | Smallest builds, native mobile, one-click Poki |

Sources for the table: [Phaser 3 vs 4](https://phaser.io/news/2026/05/phaser-3-vs-phaser-4); [Phaser Box2D](https://phaser.io/news/2024/12/announcing-phaser-box2d); [PixiJS v8](https://pixijs.com/blog/pixi-v8-launches); [Defold retrospective](https://defold.com/2026/01/02/Defold-2025-Retrospective/); [Defold 1.13](https://defold.com/2026/06/22/Defold-1-13-0/); [Unity pricing](https://unity.com/pricing-updates); [Unity 2D physics API](https://docs.unity3d.com/Manual/2d-physics-api/2d-physics-api-introduction.html); [Construct store](https://www.construct.net/make-games/buy-construct-3); [Construct physics](https://www.construct.net/en/blogs/construct-official-blog-1/behavior-improvements-910); [GameMaker](https://gamemaker.io/get).

Phaser 4, stable since April 2026, is the default web-first choice; Phaser itself says "there's no reason to start on Phaser 3" ([Phaser](https://phaser.io/news/2026/05/phaser-3-vs-phaser-4)), and its official Capacitor tutorial covers the path to iOS and Android ([Phaser](https://phaser.io/tutorials/bring-your-phaser-game-to-ios-and-android-with-capacitor)). Defold is the strongest alternative for anyone who wants native mobile apps rather than a game running inside an embedded browser, and it added one-click Poki publishing in 2025 ([Defold](https://defold.com/2026/01/02/Defold-2025-Retrospective/)); Orenji Spark reported builds about 65% smaller after moving a Poki game from Unity to Defold ([GameDev.net](https://gamedev.net/news/2581-from-unity-to-defold-how-orenji-spark-rebuilt-janes-fashion-studio-for-poki/)). Godot 4.x is a good middle ground with GDScript, but **C# projects still cannot export to the web as of Godot 4.7** ([Godot docs](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/export/exporting_for_web.html)). Unity 6 remains strongest for mobile-first games with heavy ad SDKs: Unity cancelled its per-install Runtime Fee in September 2024, and the free Personal tier now covers up to $200K in revenue ([Unity](https://unity.com/en/blog/unity-is-canceling-the-runtime-fee)). Stick Fight itself was a Unity 2017 game ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/gaming/db/game/stick-fight-the-game/)).

Physics is the decision that matters most for a ragdoll game, and Box2D v3 and Rapier2D are the two serious options. Box2D 3.1 (April 2025) adds cross-platform determinism and a filter joint that stops neighboring limbs from colliding ([Box2D](https://box2d.org/documentation/md_release__notes__v310.html)); Phaser ships an MIT-licensed JavaScript port of the v3 API at about 65–70 KB ([Phaser](https://phaser.io/news/2024/12/announcing-phaser-box2d)), and Unity 6.3 and Defold now expose Box2D v3 as well. Rapier compiles Rust to WebAssembly and offers a separate `@dimforge/rapier2d-deterministic` build that produces identical results on every platform, which rollback-style online play needs ([Yarn](https://classic.yarnpkg.com/en/package/@dimforge/rapier2d)). In an August 2026 benchmark run by the maintainers of the Godot Rapier plugin — a vendor test — Godot's built-in 2D physics ran 4.8–12.6 times slower than Rapier or Box2D and **never finished the joint-grid scene, the one most like a pile of ragdolls** ([godot.rapier.rs](https://godot.rapier.rs/blog/v0-35-0)). Matter.js, a pure-JavaScript engine that comparison guides rank behind Planck.js and Rapier for joint-heavy work ([learnwithhasan](https://learnwithhasan.com/js-libraries/compare/matter-js-vs-planck-js/)), is the weakest fit for ragdolls despite its popularity in tutorials.

For animation, the cheapest and most flexible method is to draw each limb as a line or capsule between about 10 joint positions, then drive those joints with keyframed angles, inverse kinematics or physics. Stick Fight-style active ragdolls give each limb its own rigid body and hinge joint and pull each joint toward a target pose taken from a hidden, animated copy of the character ([Unity discussions](https://discussions.unity.com/t/active-ragdoll-2d/770915); [active-ragdolls](https://github.com/sergioabreu-g/active-ragdolls)). The pull is typically a spring-like controller — torque equals a stiffness constant times the angle error minus a damping constant times the angular velocity — with an extra upright torque on the torso to keep the figure standing. Spine ($69 Essential, $369 Professional) pays off only if limbs are textured images ([Spine](https://esotericsoftware.com/spine-purchase)). For keyframes, Google's free MediaPipe Pose Landmarker runs in the browser and returns 33 body landmarks from video ([Google AI Edge](https://ai.google.dev/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/pose_landmarker/web_js); [DEV Community](https://dev.to/yoshan0921/fitness-app-development-with-real-time-posture-detection-using-mediapipe-38do)), enough to film yourself throwing a punch and turn it into joint angles at no cost, while Cascadeur's indie tier, at about $8 a month, helps with physically plausible flips ([aisotools](https://aisotools.com/blog/cascadeur-review-2026)).

Online multiplayer is where small teams get hurt. Colyseus, an open-source Node.js server whose managed cloud starts at **$15 a month** with no player caps, is the practical server-authoritative option for JavaScript stacks ([Colyseus](https://www.colyseus.io/pricing)). Photon Fusion is free up to 100 concurrent users for Unity, and Photon Quantum offers deterministic rollback with built-in 2D physics, though its web support is unconfirmed ([Photon](https://www.photonengine.com/fusion/pricing); [Photon Quantum](https://www.photonengine.com/quantum)). Infrastructure can vanish: **Hathora shut down its game-server hosting on 5 May 2026**, and the strategy game Stormgate lost its online modes as a result ([TechSpot](https://www.techspot.com/news/111969-stormgate-servers-go-dark-following-ai-focused-hosting.html)). Browser rollback with physics is effectively do-it-yourself, since NetplayJS, the main JavaScript library, was last updated in October 2024 ([npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/netplayjs); [ecosyste.ms](https://awesome.ecosyste.ms/projects/github.com%2Frameshvarun%2Fnetplayjs)). Ragdoll fighters forgive approximate sync because players accept physics chaos, so the sane design for a solo developer is a server running the same physics at 20–30 ticks per second, with clients smoothing between updates. For app-store builds, an HTML5 game can be wrapped with Capacitor, monetized with AdMob or AppLovin MAX, and use RevenueCat for purchases ([Capacitor](https://capacitorjs.com/docs/v7/guides/games); [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/getting-started/installation/capacitor.md)); MAX's optimization advantage shows up mainly beyond 100K daily users ([Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/applovin-publisher-monetization-guide)).

These findings reduce to three stack recipes. For a web-first game that later moves to mobile, use TypeScript, Vite and Phaser 4 with Phaser Box2D, then a Capacitor wrapper for the stores, or Defold if native mobile builds matter more than JavaScript. For a mobile-first game, use Unity 6 Personal with its Box2D-based 2D physics and LevelPlay or AppLovin MAX for ads, or Godot with GDScript plus Rapier, or Defold, if you want $0 licensing and a viable web build. For an online ragdoll fighter, pair Phaser or PixiJS with Rapier's deterministic build and a server-authoritative Colyseus backend; Godot with Rapier and the netfox rollback addon ([netfox](https://docsearch.algolia.com/mcp/docs/repo/foxssake/netfox)) works too once you confirm the plugin's web export, and Unity with Photon Quantum fits if web can be skipped. Server costs at indie scale run from $0 on Photon's free tier through $15–$50 a month on Colyseus Cloud to $125–$250 a month for 500–1,000 concurrent users on Photon.

## A blueprint for a standout game: written ragdoll comedy, shipped web-first

**Build a written ragdoll game**: physics-driven action that follows the proven web template but whose identity comes from a premise and jokes someone actually wrote. The evidence argues for combining three ingredients. Take the mechanic from what portals already reward — a ragdoll combat, toss or movement action that players understand within five seconds, with attempts that resolve in seconds. Take the identity from what earns 95–98% approval on Steam — a satirical or fourth-wall premise in the vein of Stick It to the Stickman's corporate tower or Animator vs. Animation's creator-versus-creation, with every failure written as a distinct, collectible gag in Henry Stickmin's manner. Take the social layer from what costs least — same-device two-player from day one, plus competent bots so solo players and empty lobbies never kill the game. One concrete version, offered as an illustration rather than a tested concept: a run-based brawler set inside an animator's sketchbook, where the stickman fights doodles and the cursor page by page, each death unlocks a captioned "fail" card, and players draw and share their own pages in a level editor. It covers the comedy gap and the player-made-content gap at once, and it clears Poki's "not a clone" bar.

Spend more on feel than on art. Build damage-scaled, capped hitstop of about 50–150 ms on attacker and victim only, shortened in three- and four-player modes; add victim shake during the freeze, short directional screen shake, ragdoll knockback that carries the hit's direction, a burst of ink or particles in a contrasting color, and layered, pitch-randomized impact sound with a low thump and a high crack, plus toggles to turn off shake and flashes. Animate with a hybrid: keyframed or controller-driven moves for readable attacks, full ragdoll on hits and deaths for comedy. Keep bodies monochrome and put saturated accents on weapons, effects and teams, all inside one framing device such as notebook paper or neon on dark. Map play to one or two inputs that work identically on mouse, touch and gamepad, add generous input buffering, ship customizable button layouts at launch rather than retrofitting them as Shadow Fight 2 had to, and support both portrait and landscape, as Poki prefers.

Make fairness a marketed feature, with each choice reversing a complaint documented above. On portals, follow Poki's rules: rewarded ads for cosmetics and revives only, never to gate progress, inside a level ladder or run structure with collectible cosmetics and the weekly rotating skins Stickman Hook uses as a return trigger. On Steam, sell a $5–$12 premium version with co-op, the level editor and Workshop support, offer a free demo, and start Early Access at a low price, as Stick It to the Stickman did. On mobile, go either premium or free with a single remove-ads purchase in the $2.99–$3.99 range players already accept and cosmetic-only items — no energy timers, gem walls or subscriptions, cloud saves that work, and ad creatives that show the real game.

The recommended stack for this blueprint is TypeScript with Vite and Phaser 4; Phaser Box2D for single-player and local play, or Rapier2D's deterministic build if online play is planned; procedurally drawn stick figures keyed from a MediaPipe-to-joint-angle pipeline; and an initial download under 5–8 MB with everything else lazy-loaded. Put ads and purchases behind one interface so the Poki, CrazyGames, Capacitor-store and Steam builds differ only in small adapters. Choose Defold instead if native mobile performance and the smallest build matter more than JavaScript familiarity, or Godot with GDScript and the Rapier plugin if you want a full editor — but first verify that the plugin exports to the web, because its benchmark post doesn't mention web. Avoid Matter.js and Godot's built-in 2D physics for ragdolls, Godot C# for anything bound for the web, Unity if iOS web reach matters, and any multiplayer host you cannot replace. When online play arrives, run it server-authoritative on Colyseus with bots filling empty slots, and own the servers and publishing rights; Stick Fight Mobile's disappearance shows what happens to a popular game that doesn't.

| Phase | Where | Scope | Revenue model | Gate to advance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Prototype | Private playtests and an unlisted page only | One action, one arena, local 2P, full impact effects, fail cards | None | Strangers replay without being asked; a 10-second clip is funny without explanation |
| 2. Web launch | Poki web exclusive (or CrazyGames, non-exclusive) | Run or level structure, bots, cosmetics, weekly skins, under 8 MB initial load | Portal ads, rewarded only for cosmetics | Pass Poki's Player Fit and Web Fit tests, or CrazyGames' Basic Launch into Full Launch |
| 3. Premium PC | Steam (Early Access, then 1.0) | Expanded campaign, co-op, level editor, Workshop, free demo | $5–$12 premium | Review score and recent-review trend |
| 4. Mobile | iOS and Android via Capacitor (or Defold native) | Touch-tuned controls, cloud save, honest store creatives | Premium, or free with one-time remove-ads and cosmetics | Top-quartile retention: about 30% day 1, 6–7% day 7 |
| 5. Online | All platforms | Server-authoritative PvP with bot backfill and profanity-filtered usernames | Cosmetics | Lobbies stay full without cheating complaints |

Keep the prototype off public web portals if you want Poki's revenue-share deal, because Poki offers games already live on other web platforms only a one-time flat licence fee with no revenue share ([Poki deal types](https://developers.poki.com/guide/revenue-deal-types)). Build your own audience as well: Poki pays 100% of revenue on players you bring yourself ([Poki Developer Guide](https://developers.poki.com/guide)), so every YouTube or TikTok follower raises your effective cut. Pick a distinctive, trademark-checked name rather than fighting for the bare "stickman" keyword, and target longer store phrases instead. Build clip tools into the game — instant replay of the last few seconds, a vertical-friendly camera option and shareable level codes — because Landfall's experience shows physics games spread when one clip conveys the whole game. Use the portals' own tests to decide whether to continue: Poki's Player Fit Test returns real player data within hours (one developer's account puts the bar near 25% of 500 testers playing three minutes or more, an anecdote rather than a published rule; [Cinevva](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/publish-game-poki)), and CrazyGames' Basic Launch benchmarks playtime, conversion and retention ([CrazyGames docs](https://docs.crazygames.com/)). On mobile, aim for top-quartile retention of about 30% on day 1 and 6–7% on day 7, and adopt a recurring event, as Landfall did with April Fools, so the community has a date to come back for.

## Conclusion

Mechanics are not scarce in the stickman market — ragdoll duels, rope swings and idle battlers are cloned by the hundred. What is scarce is a distinct identity and players' trust. The best-loved games in the genre, Henry Stickmin at 98% and Stick It to the Stickman at 95%, are the ones with a writer's voice and no ads, while the biggest free games average 4.5 stars over a seething tail of monetization complaints. That gap is the opening. A small team cannot outspend Fansipan on user acquisition or outrank Stick War: Legacy's 3.2M ratings, but it can be funnier and fairer, and stick figures — the cheapest bodies in games to animate and simulate — free the budget to do exactly that.

The platforms are better seen as a ladder than as a choice. Web portals offer free reach and fast player tests but pay modestly in ads; Steam turns a known game into premium revenue; mobile delivers scale mainly to those who already have a brand or a user-acquisition budget. Henry Stickmin, Fancy Pants, Stick War and Ericetto's ragdoll games each climbed some version of this ladder, and Poki's web-only exclusivity is written to allow it. With Stick Fight Mobile's slot still empty and 2025–26's best-reviewed stickman games absent from phones, the top rung on mobile is open to whoever builds a durable, fair, online-capable physics game — and after Hathora and Stick Fight Mobile, durability is itself a feature worth designing for.
