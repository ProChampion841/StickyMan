# Web/Browser and PC (Steam) Stickman 2D Game Market (as of October 2026)

Research date: 2026-10-15. All Poki/CrazyGames/Steam figures below were read directly from live pages on that date unless otherwise labeled. **Important caveat on metrics:** Poki and CrazyGames do NOT display play counts on game pages; the visible popularity signals are rating vote counts (Poki: "X votes" + likes/dislikes; CrazyGames: likes/dislikes and a 10-point rating "based on last 6 months"). Vote counts are a proxy for reach, not plays. Newgrounds, itch.io (403), Y8 (404), Armor Games, Kongregate and CoolMathGames pages could not be retrieved, so those portals are thinly covered (see Gaps).

## 1. What are the most-played stickman games on the major web portals and Steam?

### Takeaway
On Poki, Stickman Hook (Madbox) is in a different league from everything else, with about 7.9M rating votes. Next come the Henry Stickmin games (Fleeing the Complex, about 2.4M votes), Stick Merge (about 1.9M) and War of Sticks (about 950K). On CrazyGames, Ragdoll Archers (Ericetto) leads with about 2.7M likes plus dislikes. On Steam, the two flagship stickman hits are Stick Fight: The Game (about 52.7K English reviews; Landfall said it sold over 2M copies by 2019) and The Henry Stickmin Collection (98% positive of about 41K English reviews). The 2025–26 newcomer is Stick It to the Stickman (Free Lives/Devolver).

## 2. Which stickman sub-genres dominate the web?

### Takeaway
Six formats account for nearly all high-vote web stickman games:
- **Ragdoll-physics fighting/action with same-keyboard 2-player** is the dominant new-release format from 2023 to 2026.
- **One-button physics skill games** (Stickman Hook) are the single biggest title.
- **Henry Stickmin-style choose-your-path comedy** gets the highest approval.
- **Stick War-style army/lane strategy** remains strong.
- **Merge/idle shooters and defenders** are a durable mid-tier.
- **Parkour/momentum platformers** (Fancy Pants, Vex, the Stickman Parkour series) fill out the long tail.

Hypercasual crowd runners (Count Masters) cross over from mobile.

### Inferences
- Ragdoll fighting is both the hottest and the most crowded web sub-genre. At least 7 titles with 190K+ votes each launched between 2022 and 2025. Poki states that "Clones of existing hits ... won't be promoted," so a new entry needs a clear twist.
- Local 2-player on one keyboard is effectively table stakes for web stickman fighters. It is cheap to build (no netcode) and fits school/couch play.
- There is a gap where no current web title occupies the space between the narrative appeal of Henry Stickmin and the replayable mechanical loop of ragdoll fighters. No recent narrative/comedy stickman breakout appeared in the data.

### Gaps

## 3. Portal requirements and economics for a small developer (Poki, CrazyGames, itch.io, Newgrounds)

### Takeaway
Poki is a curated, mostly-mobile, 100M-monthly-player platform. It pays 50% of ad revenue on Poki-driven traffic and 100% on traffic you bring. In return it asks for 5-year web-only exclusivity, which leaves Steam, mobile and console open. It wants under-10-second loads, touch plus mouse controls and an original core loop. CrazyGames (about 35–50M+ monthly players) is non-exclusive and gated by a metrics-based Basic → Full Launch process. Its revenue share is not published (about 60% ads / 70% IAP in 2026 jam terms). itch.io is self-serve with a developer-set revenue share. Reliable per-game earnings data is scarce; secondary guides suggest a few hundred to a few thousand USD per month for a solid casual web game.

### Cited Findings

**Poki: scale**
- Poki reports 100M monthly players, 1B+ gameplays per month, 500+ partner developers, "5M+ monthly players per high-performing game," and "#1 on web" in 100+ countries. — [Poki for Developers](https://developers.poki.com/)
- Poki welcomed 625M players across desktop and mobile in 2025. — [CB Insights: Poki](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/poki)

**Poki: revenue and deal types**
- Players who arrive directly (bookmarks, search, social, your own community) earn the developer 100% of revenue. Players arriving via Poki.com or Poki marketing are split 50/50. — [Poki Developer Guide](https://developers.poki.com/guide)
- Deal types: "Web exclusive (preferred)" runs **5 years by default**. Discord and YouTube Playables count as web; Steam, mobile app stores and consoles are not part of the exclusivity. Non-exclusive deals (for games already live on other web platforms, or a niche fit) are "a one-time flat license fee instead, with no revenue share." The terms are "indicative" and set per agreement. — [Poki: Revenue deal types](https://developers.poki.com/guide/revenue-deal-types)
- One secondary search summary claimed an older Poki bonus page listed 7-year exclusivity. That URL now redirects to the deal-types page, which says 5 years, so treat 5 years as current. — [Poki deal types](https://developers.poki.com/guide/revenue-deal-types)
- Payouts and monetization: no in-app purchases or purchases for currency or ad removal; Poki's SDK is the only ad system allowed; rewarded videos can't gate core progress. — [Poki requirements](https://developers.poki.com/guide/requirements-quality)

**Poki: what it looks for (official)**
- The core criteria are quality (UX/feel and core loop), player fit for a "global, mostly mobile audience," and tech ("fast load times, stable frame rates, and a lean file size").
- "Clones of existing hits don't stand out to players and won't be promoted."
- "A satisfying core loop that opens up over time holds attention far better than a single clever idea."
- "Players decide in seconds, so a clear thumbnail and an immediate, playable opening matter."
- "Controls and UI that work as well with a thumb as with a mouse." Also: "a desktop-only game is at a disadvantage."
- Poki favors games with "room to grow" (content hooks, update-ready systems).
- No single criterion is pass/fail.
- Source for all of the above: [Poki: What we look for](https://developers.poki.com/guide/what-we-look-for)

**Poki: technical requirements (official)**
- "Players tend to move to another game if loading takes more than 10 seconds."
- 16:9, scaling to 640x360, 836x470 or 1031x580. Must support desktop, mobile and tablet, filling the screen in portrait and/or landscape ("both is best"); tablets must force mobile controls.
- Must work in incognito (wrap localStorage in try/catch) and with ad blockers. External requests are blocked, so all assets must be bundled.
- SDK: `gameplayStart()` fires on first input, `gameplayStop()` on pause, menu or level end, and `commercialBreak()` only when returning to gameplay.
- Rewarded-ad button rules: a standard continue button is always offered alongside and at least as large; reward buttons can't be green and must show a 🎬 icon.
- Static and animated thumbnails are required. A profanity filter is required for multiplayer games with usernames. Localization is recommended and cutscenes must be skippable. No splash screens beyond a studio logo on the loading screen.
- Source for all of the above: [Poki: Requirements & quality](https://developers.poki.com/guide/requirements-quality)
- The current official page states no MB cap. A third-party guide cites an earlier Poki requirement of an initial download under 8 MB. — [Cinevva Poki guide](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/publish-game-poki)

**Poki: process**
- The process runs: playtests and Player Fit Tests (real player data "in just a few hours"), then Web Fit Test, final review, soft release and global release. "We keep daily releases limited, so each game gets a real shot at being seen." — [Poki Developer Guide](https://developers.poki.com/guide)
- Secondary details: Web Fit Test takes about 3–5 days. Soft release takes about 1–2 weeks to schedule and then runs 2–3 weeks. Soft-release ad guidance is at least 1 ad per daily active user (citing Poki's release-process docs). One developer's account puts the Player Fit Test bar at about 25% of 500 testers playing at least 3 minutes (anecdotal). — [Cinevva Poki guide](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/publish-game-poki)
- Poki publishes a live "Player device report" sampled from 100M players and updated daily (Oct 2026), but its chart data did not load in my fetch. — [Poki device report](https://developers.poki.com/guide/player-device-report)
- Platform features for multiplayer and persistence: Netlib (networking), AUDS and User Accounts. — [Poki Developer Guide](https://developers.poki.com/guide)

**CrazyGames: launch process (official)**
- **Basic Launch** runs 7–21 days with a limited audience. No SDK is needed and ads, banners and IAP are disabled. It ends once the game has been live at least 7 days and reached at least 500 plays, or automatically after 21 days.
- Progression to **Full Launch** depends on average playtime, conversion to gameplay and retention, benchmarked against other games. Full Launch requires full SDK integration and QA, and enables monetization and revenue share.
- Failing most metrics means resubmitting as a new game. Multiplayer titles needing a larger audience, and invited games already published elsewhere, may skip Basic Launch.
- SDKs exist for HTML5, Unity, GameMaker, Construct 3, Godot and Cocos, plus third-party integrations for GDevelop, Defold and Wonderland. SDK modules cover video ads, banners, game, user, data, IAP and leaderboards, with cloud save and friend invites. Revenue comes from ads, plus IAP for selected games only.
- Source for all of the above: [CrazyGames docs](https://docs.crazygames.com/)

**CrazyGames: technical requirements (official)**
- Total build at most 250 MB and at most 1,500 files. Initial download at most 50 MB, and **at most 20 MB for the mobile homepage**. Externally loaded games need time to gameplay of 20 seconds or less.
- Must run on Chrome/Edge; Safari-poor games may be disabled there. Chromebooks require smooth play on a 4 GB RAM device. Unity games are disabled on iOS by default.
- Desktop must be playable in landscape; portrait is allowed with side bars. Ads are not allowed in Basic Launch.
- Source for all of the above: [CrazyGames technical requirements](https://docs.crazygames.com/requirements/technical/)

**CrazyGames: revenue and payouts (mostly secondary)**
- The revenue split is not published in the main docs. The 2026 GameMaker web jam terms list 60% of ad revenue and 70% of IAP revenue to developers (jam-specific).
- IAP is invite-only via Xsolla.
- Payouts are monthly via Tipalti at a €100 minimum, with balances rolling over.
- No exclusivity required, per the guide's reading of CrazyGames docs.
- Hands-on SDK and ad-placement support starts after 50,000 combined plays.
- Clones, reskins and asset flips are screened out.
- Source for all of the above: [Cinevva CrazyGames guide](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/publish-game-crazygames)
- Scale figures conflict:
  - The developer portal claims 50M+ monthly players and 300M+ sessions per month (per the guide).
  - Mobidictum reported 35M MAU in early 2025.
  - A 2023 press release said 25M+ monthly players and "millions of dollars to developers in revenue share each year."
  - Sources: [Cinevva CrazyGames guide](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/publish-game-crazygames); [Cinevva monetization guide](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/web-game-monetization)
- Historical (October 2018): CrazyGames had about 10M monthly users and 4 employees, 2017 turnover of €1.02M, and launched its developer portal with opt-in revenue share. — [Start it @KBC press release](https://start-it-x.prezly.com/crazygames-launches-new-developer-portal-with-revenue-share-options)

**Other portals and cross-portal comparisons**
- **itch.io:** the default developer share is about 90%, since itch.io's revenue share is set by the developer. — [Cinevva web game monetization](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/web-game-monetization)
- **Earnings benchmarks (weak, secondary):**
  - "a solid casual game on a major portal" earns about $200–$2,000 per month. — [Cinevva web game monetization](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/web-game-monetization)
  - About $500–$3,000 per month for a first web game on Poki, citing indiegamebusiness.com. — [Cinevva Poki guide](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/publish-game-poki)
  - Web ad-rate benchmarks are "thin" because most published data is for mobile apps. — [Cinevva monetization (DE)](https://app.cinevva.com/de/guides/web-game-monetization)
- **Competitor view (Playgama, which discloses its interest):** Poki, CrazyGames and GameDistribution do not publish universal splits; terms are negotiated per game. A high-percentage exclusive deal can pay less over a year than a lower-percentage non-exclusive one. — [Playgama](https://playgama.com/blog/business-faqs/poki-vs-crazygames-vs-gamedistribution-revenue-share/)

### Inferences
- Poki's exclusivity covers only the open web, so a solo developer can do a Poki web exclusive and still ship on Steam and mobile. This matches the Henry Stickmin and Fancy Pants path (free web game, then a paid Steam/console version).
- The binding technical constraints for a mobile-capable web stickman game:
  - Under 10 seconds to playable on Poki.
  - At most 20 MB initial download to reach CrazyGames' mobile homepage.
  - Touch controls as good as mouse/keyboard.
  - Landscape plus portrait support.
  - Lean 2D vector/sprite art (a natural fit for stickman games) helps on all four.
- Both portals gate on early engagement metrics (Poki Player Fit/Web Fit tests, CrazyGames Basic Launch playtime, conversion and retention). The first 30–60 seconds of play and the thumbnail matter disproportionately.
- Poki bans IAP and CrazyGames' IAP is invite-only, so web stickman revenue is essentially ad revenue (rewarded plus midroll). Rewarded-ad hooks (skins, revives, weapons) must be designed in without gating progress.

## 4. Iconic Flash-era stickman titles: what made them beloved, and what are their modern successors?

### Takeaway
The Flash-era stickman canon grew mostly out of Newgrounds:
- **Xiao Xiao** (2001–02) popularized stick-figure kung-fu animation.
- **Stick RPG** (2003) got 1M plays in its first month and founded XGen Studios.
- **Fancy Pants** (2006–) brought momentum platforming with tens of millions of portal plays.
- **Henry Stickmin** (2008–) made fail-state comedy famous.
- **Stick War** (2009) set the template for army strategy.

The strongest modern successors are the same IPs re-released: Henry Stickmin on Steam and Poki, Fancy Pants on Poki and Steam, Stick War on CrazyGames via Ruffle and on mobile. Imitators (War of Sticks, the ragdoll fighters) continue the gameplay lines.

### Cited Findings
- **Xiao Xiao** (Zhu Zhiqiang, Beijing): 10 short (1–2 minute) Flash cartoons on Newgrounds, released April 19, 2001 to February 23, 2002. Some installments were interactive and game-like. Other creators made sequels and parodies, and "Xiao Xiao" became a name for the anonymous black stick figure. Wikipedia lists no view counts. — [Wikipedia: Xiao Xiao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xiao_Xiao)
- **Stick RPG** (Skye Boyes, 2003, Flash): "one million plays in the first month." Boyes then dropped out of university and incorporated XGen Studios, whose follow-up was Motherload (2004). Later came Stick RPG: Complete (2005, browser) and Stick RPG 2: Director's Cut (2014, Windows/Mac). — [Wikipedia: XGen Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/XGen_Studios); [MobyGames: XGen](https://www.mobygames.com/company/10830/xgen-studios-inc/)
- **Stick Arena** (XGen): free online multiplayer Flash shooter, later expanded as Stick Arena Ballistick. XGen brought classics including Stick Arena to Steam. Its original release year was not confirmed. — [Giant Bomb: XGen](https://giantbomb.com/wiki/Companies/XGen_Studios_Inc); [MobyGames](https://www.mobygames.com/company/10830/xgen-studios-inc/)
- **Fancy Pants Adventures** (Brad Borne): World 1 on March 14, 2006; World 2 on January 9, 2008; World 3 on April 5, 2012. Console versions (EA 2D) came out in 2011, Super Fancy Pants Adventure on Steam on September 20, 2017, and the Classic Pack in Steam Early Access on February 18, 2024.
  - Play counts as of May 2011: World 1 about 7M on Armor Games, 7.8M on Addicting Games and 2.3M+ on Kongregate. World 2 about 14.8M on Armor Games, 17.8M on Addicting Games and 5M+ on Kongregate.
  - World 2 won the 2008 Newgrounds Tank Award for Best Flash Game and was the #5 most-played Flash game of 2008 on Mochi.
  - Why it was loved: Sonic-like momentum platforming, collectibles and side goals (Snail Shell Golf unlocking pants colors), and community beta testing via the developer's blog.
  - Source for all of the above: [Wikipedia: Fancy Pants Adventures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fancy_Pants_Adventures)
  - Fancy Pants 1, 2 and 3 are currently on Poki's stickman page. — [Poki Stickman](https://poki.com/en/stickman)
- **Henry Stickmin** (Marcus Bromander / PuffballsUnited):
  - Breaking the Bank premiered on Newgrounds on August 27, 2008. Stealing the Diamond followed in 2011, Infiltrating the Airship in 2013 and Fleeing the Complex in 2015.
  - Every installment from the second onward reportedly hit the Newgrounds front page and topped Weekly Users' Choice.
  - Bromander co-founded Innersloth (Among Us) with Forest Willard in 2015. Among Us's Airship map (March 31, 2021) is based on the Henry Stickmin Toppat airship.
  - Sources: [Meme.com: Henry Stickmin](https://meme.com/memes/henry-stickmin); [Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/subcultures/the-henry-stickmin-collection). These are wiki/meme sources, so treat specific dates as moderately reliable.
- Henry Stickmin's appeal per Steam: multiple fail endings ("failing is more fun than succeeding"), choose-your-own-path, meme and pop-culture parody. Steam tags are Funny, Multiple Endings, Memes and Comedy. — [Steam: Henry Stickmin Collection](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1089980/The_Henry_Stickmin_Collection/)
- **Stick War** (Brock White programming, Jason Whitham animation): uploaded to Newgrounds on August 25, 2009 as "whiting39." It made the front page the same day and was Daily Feature the next day (per fan wiki). It continued on mobile as "Stick War: Legacy." The original is still playable via Ruffle on CrazyGames, rated 9.4 with 152K votes. — [Stick War Fandom](https://stick-war.fandom.com/wiki/Brock_White/whiting39); [CrazyGames: Stick War](https://www.crazygames.com/game/stick-war)
- **Flash preservation:** Newgrounds sponsored the Ruffle emulator in 2019. Armor Games announced Ruffle use in December 2020, though many Flash games remain inaccessible. Ruffle covers AS1/AS2 better than AS3. — [Wikipedia: Ruffle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruffle_(software)); [Archive Team: Newgrounds](https://wiki.archiveteam.org/index.php/Newgrounds)

### Inferences
- **What made them beloved:**
  - A strong authorial voice and humor (Henry Stickmin).
  - Depth surprising for a free game (Stick RPG's open-ended sandbox; Fancy Pants' momentum and secrets).
  - Martial-arts choreography spectacle (Xiao Xiao).
  - Satisfying strategic escalation (Stick War).
  - Each was the work of one person or a tiny team and spread through Newgrounds front-page and portal virality.
- **Modern successor map:**
  - Henry Stickmin → the Steam Collection plus Poki/CrazyGames re-hosting, with no true new successor.
  - Stick War → War of Sticks (Poki, 950K votes) and Stick War: Legacy (mobile).
  - Xiao Xiao / Electric Man-style fighting → ragdoll fighters (Stick Fighter, Ragdoll Hit, Stickman Battle) and Steam's Stick Fight and Stick It to the Stickman.
  - Fancy Pants → itself, on Poki plus Steam.
  - Stick RPG → no obvious web successor found (a possible open niche).
- The Henry Stickmin and Fancy Pants paths show a free web series building an audience that later converted to paid Steam/console sales.

### Gaps
- **Electric Man** and **Stickman Madness**: no reliable sources found on developer, dates or plays.
- Original Newgrounds view counts and scores for the Henry Stickmin games, Xiao Xiao and Stick War could not be retrieved (403).
- No verified Wikipedia article was retrieved for Henry Stickmin or Stick War (404s at guessed URLs).

## 5. Notable 2024–2026 web stickman breakouts and trend shifts

### Takeaway
Recent breakouts are overwhelmingly ragdoll-physics fighters or action games with local 2-player: Ragdoll Hit, Stickman Clash, Stickman Kombat 2D, Stickman Battle, Ragdoll Drop and Mad Stick. They come from small studios or serial solo-ish developers such as Ericetto. Trends point toward:
- online multiplayer layered over local play (Stickman Battle, Stickman Clash planning 3–4 players);
- web games shipping simultaneously into portal mobile apps and app stores;
- portals explicitly favoring mobile-first, touch-friendly, fast-loading builds.

On Steam, Stick It to the Stickman (2025 Early Access → 1.0 in September 2026) is the notable new stickman hit.

### Cited Findings
- **Ragdoll Hit** (Ericetto, May 2024): 611K votes on Poki. — [Poki](https://poki.com/en/g/ragdoll-hit)
- **Stickman Clash** (December 2024): 764K likes plus dislikes on CrazyGames. Still updated in September 2026, with 3–4 player modes planned. — [CrazyGames](https://www.crazygames.com/game/stickman-clash)
- **Stickman Kombat 2D** (GamePush, August 2025): 294K likes plus dislikes, rated 9.0. — [CrazyGames](https://www.crazygames.com/game/stickman-kombat-2d)
- **Stickman Battle** (EasyCats, November 2025): 410K votes within about 11 months. #3 on Poki's stickman page. Local 2-player plus online play and leaderboard. — [Poki](https://poki.com/en/g/stickman-battle)
- **Ragdoll Drop** (Ericetto, November 2025): 193K votes at a 4.6 rating. — [Poki](https://poki.com/en/g/ragdoll-drop)
- **Mad Stick** (FPDA, September 2026): already #7 on Poki's stickman page with 8.7K votes. — [Poki](https://poki.com/en/g/mad-stick)
- **Serial-hit developer Ericetto:** Ragdoll Archers (CrazyGames, 2023, about 2.7M votes), Ragdoll Hit (Poki, 2024, 611K) and Ragdoll Drop (Poki, 2025, 193K). Ragdoll Archers was also released on Android in September 2023 and listed on Google Play, Microsoft Store and the CrazyGames iOS/Android app. — [CrazyGames: Ragdoll Archers](https://www.crazygames.com/game/ragdoll-archers); [Poki: Ragdoll Hit](https://poki.com/en/g/ragdoll-hit)
- **Web-to-app distribution:**
  - CrazyGames lists Ragdoll Archers, Stickman Clash and Stickman Kombat 2D as available in the "CrazyGames App (iOS, Android)." Its technical docs require whitelisting the iOS/Android app origins and set a ≤20 MB initial-download bar for the mobile homepage. — [CrazyGames tech requirements](https://docs.crazygames.com/requirements/technical/)
  - Poki counts Discord and YouTube Playables as "web" under exclusivity, signaling those as distribution surfaces. — [Poki deal types](https://developers.poki.com/guide/revenue-deal-types)
- **Mobile-first portal stance:** Poki's audience is "mostly mobile," and "a desktop-only game is at a disadvantage." — [Poki: What we look for](https://developers.poki.com/guide/what-we-look-for)
- **Multiplayer infrastructure on portals:**
  - Poki offers Netlib networking and User Accounts. — [Poki Developer Guide](https://developers.poki.com/guide)
  - CrazyGames lets "multiplayer titles needing a larger audience" skip Basic Launch and offers friend invites and leaderboards in its SDK. — [CrazyGames docs](https://docs.crazygames.com/)
- **Steam:** Stick It to the Stickman (Free Lives/Devolver) launched in Early Access on August 18, 2025 and hit 1.0 on September 23, 2026. It is at 95% of 5,199 English reviews, though recent reviews dipped to 81%. It is a physics brawler with couch co-op, a level editor and Workshop. — [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2085540/Stick_It_to_the_Stickman/)
- Stick Fight is still selling 8 years after launch: 247 reviews in the last 30 days at 92%. — [Steam: Stick Fight](https://store.steampowered.com/app/674940/Stick_Fight_The_Game/)
- **Engines in use among the hits:** Stickman Clash uses Unity 2021; Ragdoll Archers and Stickman Kombat 2D are HTML5. CrazyGames disables Unity on iOS by default, which favors lightweight HTML5 engines for mobile web reach. — [CrazyGames: Stickman Clash](https://www.crazygames.com/game/stickman-clash); [CrazyGames tech requirements](https://docs.crazygames.com/requirements/technical/)

### Inferences
- The 2024–2026 winning web formula looks like this:
  - A ragdoll physics stickman.
  - An instantly readable combat or toss mechanic.
  - Weapons and skins as progression and rewarded-ad hooks.
  - Same-keyboard 2-player.
  - Desktop plus mobile touch.
  - Steady updates.
- New entrants can still reach hundreds of thousands of votes within a year (Stickman Battle, Ragdoll Drop). This suggests the category is not closed, but differentiation and quality bar are rising (Poki's anti-clone stance).
- A standout game could combine the proven web mechanics (ragdoll combat with local 2P/online) with something the current field lacks:
  - Henry Stickmin-style authored humor and narrative.
  - Stick RPG-style open-ended depth.
  - Stick Fight-style party chaos with level creation/sharing.
- The proven expansion path is web first (Poki exclusive is web-only), then Steam with a premium or expanded version and possibly mobile. This is the path Henry Stickmin, Fancy Pants and Stick War each followed in some form.
- Lightweight HTML5 (or a tightly optimized engine build) is advantageous given the ≤20 MB mobile homepage and under-10-second load targets and Unity-on-iOS restrictions.

### Gaps
- No portal-published "top games of 2025" list for stickman specifically was found, so breakout identification relies on release dates and vote counts on live pages.
- No public developer postmortem from Ericetto, EasyCats, GamePush, TinyDobbins, Madbox (on the web side) or Amazing Adam was found. Revenue for these web hits is unknown.
- I found no data on cross-play between web and mobile versions of the same stickman game. Ragdoll Archers ships to both, but whether players can play each other was not confirmed.
- The mobile vs desktop traffic split on Poki and CrazyGames was not retrievable as a number.
